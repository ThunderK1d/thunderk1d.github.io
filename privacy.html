Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

AutoPoster is a personal desktop application that runs only on its owner's computer and uploads the owner's videos to the owner's YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts. This policy explains what data the application accesses and how it is handled.

Data the application accesses

  • Google / YouTube: permission to upload videos to the connected YouTube channel (youtube.upload) and to read the channel name to confirm which channel is connected (youtube.readonly).
  • TikTok: basic profile information (display name) and permission to upload and publish videos to the connected account.
  • Instagram: basic profile information (username, account ID) and permission to publish Reels to the connected professional account.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by these platforms.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the owner's videos, with their titles and descriptions, to the owner's accounts and to show which account is connected. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage

Tokens and upload history are stored only in files on the owner's own computer. AutoPoster has no server, does not collect data, and does not transmit data to anyone other than the platform APIs listed above.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties.

Google API Services

AutoPoster's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

AutoPoster uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions
  • Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites

Deleting the application folder on the computer removes all locally stored tokens and history.
